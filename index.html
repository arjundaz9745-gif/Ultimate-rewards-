Frequently Asked Questions

How do I order?

Select an offer and create an order ticket.

Do customers need to register?

No. Customers can order or contact support without a website account.

How is staff registration restricted?

Staff use Discord OAuth. The server verifies the Discord account is in the official server and has the required staff role before granting staff access.

Can normal members register as staff?

No. Members without the required staff role are denied staff access.

What if payment fails?

Create a support ticket with your Discord username, product and issue. Never send passwords, OTPs or secret keys.

Your Ultimate Rewards Account
